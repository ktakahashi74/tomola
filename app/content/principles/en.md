# tomola's principles

tomola is a place to exchange ideas across languages. People who use different languages have often read different things and start from different assumptions. tomola is where those differences meet.

On tomola, each of us can test our own thinking and notice new ways of seeing things. It does not aim to merge opinions into a single consensus. What matters is that different views stay visible side by side, and that meeting them can move our thinking.

This page first explains what you can do on tomola. Then it explains, in order, the principles tomola keeps.

## 1. What you can do on tomola

- **Read, write and respond.** On tomola, essays, explainers and other things to read are published as posts. You can write a post in your own language. Posts are translated into the other languages. You can read in your own language and search in your own language. You can reply to a post whatever its language. A reply can be short. Your username appears on your posts and replies. It does not have to be your real name.
- **Mark a post "Insightful".** When a post gives you a new way of seeing something, you can mark it "Insightful". This is not the same as agreeing. The names of people who mark a post are not shown. Only the count is visible.
- **Ask for positions, and choose one.** A person who posts can ask readers for their position. For example, they can make "A picture made by AI can be called art" a post that asks for positions. Readers can choose "agree", "disagree" or "neither". On this page, a post that asks for positions is called a "question".
- **Positions are counted without names.** Members see only how many people chose each position. Who chose which position is not shown on the screens other people see or in the data AI reads. The position you chose is kept as your own record, so that you can reconsider it later. The operator does not look into that record either. You can withdraw your position or delete the record at any time.
- **Write your reasons, or an objection.** You can write the reasons for your position as a reply. Your username appears on replies. If you object to how the question itself is framed, you can write that objection as a reply. You can also post a question of your own that frames it differently.
- **Anyone can read. You log in to write.** People who are not logged in can also read posts and their translations. You need to log in to write, search, mark a post "Insightful" or choose a position.

## 2. The original is the source

- **Posts are translated into all the languages available on tomola.** Translations are made by AI.
- **A translation does not replace the original.** The original is always one click away.
- **Each language has moderators.** They correct translations in that language, decide how terms are translated, and handle reports. The operator asks members of that language to do this. You can object to their decisions.
- **tomola is open to every language.** Every language that has people who want to join is included. But to make a language usable, it must have moderators. Languages become usable one by one, as moderators are found for them.
- **Problems with a translation can be reported.** Any member can report one. A reported translation is retranslated. If that does not fix it, the moderators for that language correct it.
- **How terms are translated is collected in a glossary.** It covers terms specific to the field (for example "alignment"). Any member can read the glossary. If you want to change how a term is translated, you can propose it as a post. The moderators for that language decide. The history of changes is open to members.

## 3. How posts are ordered and evaluated on tomola

- **The rule for each order is the same for everyone.** You choose which order to read in. We do not use an algorithm that orders posts differently for each person.
- **We do not make recommendations whose only purpose is to keep you on the site longer.**
- **We do not rate people with a combined score.** We do not combine several measures into a score such as "this person scores so many points".

## 4. Minority positions and objections stay visible

- **Reasons for every position are shown at the same size.** A question page has a section that lists reasons for "agree", "disagree" and "neither". Reasons for a position few people chose are as visible as reasons for a position many chose. A position with no reasons yet is shown empty.
- **The reasons to show are not picked by the number of "Insightful" marks.** They are picked by drawing lots, in a way that keeps the languages the reasons were written in from repeating as far as possible. This is so that reasons from people of various languages appear side by side. After that, reasons that have at least one "Insightful" mark are given priority.
- **The reasons shown are the same for everyone who looks.** They change once a day.
- **Objections to a question stay visible too.** Objections to how a question is framed, and questions that frame it differently, are always shown on the question page.

## 5. Whoever posts takes responsibility for what they post

- **You can use tomola together with your own AI.** Your own AI means the AI you usually use. That includes chat AIs, agent AIs that carry out tasks following your instructions, and the more autonomous AIs that will appear in the future. Your AI can read posts on tomola. It can also write a draft and send it to your drafts. A draft is not visible to others until you post it. Any AI works. tomola does not choose one for you.
- **You can write it yourself, or write it with AI.** Whether to use AI, and what to put forward as your own thinking, are yours to decide.
- **What people do, and what AI can do.** AI can read, search, compare translations with originals, and write drafts. Publishing, choosing a position, marking a post "Insightful", reporting a problem and making an objection are things people do.
- **Only what you have checked appears under your name.** A draft written by AI becomes your post when you read it and post it. AI can read and write, but you are the one who takes responsibility.
- **AI cannot speak for itself yet.** In the future, tomola is considering letting your AI answer questions addressed to you, speaking as "the AI of (your name)". When that happens, it will carry a mark that shows it comes from AI.
- **We do not treat AI output as correct.** tomola uses AI too, for translation and search. Translations and search results made by AI are shown as coming from AI. tomola does not endorse them as correct. We also never have AI merge everyone's opinions into one.
- **People decide three things.** What to ask. Which ideas to hold as your own. What tomola's rules should be. People are the ones who live with the results.
- **You can have your AI choose what you read first.** Even then, you can always read the reasons of people in other languages, and of people who disagree with you, directly.

## 6. No one should be able to control the site unilaterally

- The operator, the people of any one language, and AI: tomola's goal is that none of them can control the site unilaterally.
- **Reports and moderation.** A member who thinks a post breaks the participation rules can tell the operator and the moderators for the language of that post. We call this a report. Who made a report is not shown to the moderators or in the public record. The operator and the moderators may hide posts that break the participation rules, such as harassment or spam, or suspend a participant. We call this moderation. The record of moderation is open to members. The participation rules are collected on a page separate from this one. {{principles-rules-status}}
- **Objections to moderation.** A person who is subject to moderation can object. A reviewer, not the person who moderated, reviews the objection. If the objection is accepted, the moderation is reversed. The operator asks someone other than the operator to be the reviewer.
- **Objections to principles and rules.** Anyone can also object to what is written on this page (the principles and the rules for ordering posts). You can also object to a decision, made by the moderators for a language, on how a term is translated. The reviewer looks at these objections too.
- **Review.** What is written on this page is reviewed on a schedule.
- **Freedom to leave.** You can delete your own posts at any time. A deleted post, including its translations, is no longer visible to anyone. You can also take your data with you, and you can leave tomola.

## 7. How to object

- You can object to moderation through the link in the notice you receive.
