# The name tomola

Unverified machine translation

I chose the name tomola so that it could be said easily in as many languages as possible. Each of its three sounds, to, mo and la, pairs one consonant with one vowel, and sounds of this kind are found in almost every language.

The name can also be read as a meaningful word in several languages.

Tomo comes from the Greek tomos, which originally meant "a piece cut off". Through the Latin tomus, it came to mean a volume of a book in many languages, such as tomo in Spanish, Italian and Portuguese, том (tom) in Russian and tom in Polish. Each post on tomola, too, is a volume of its own.

La points to a place: it means "there" as là in French and Italian and lá in Portuguese, and "at" or "to" in Romanian. I built tomola so that people coming from any language can talk in the same place.

In Japanese, tomo means a friend (友), and also "together" (共, as in 共に). Because Japanese does not distinguish the sounds l and r, tomola can also be heard as 友ら, "friends".

If you notice how the name reads in another language, please let us know in a post.

Koichi Takahashi (initiator of tomola), October 2026
