## 2. Information collected

**Article reads**: We count site-wide daily totals by original language, reading language, source category (such as search, social networks or shared links) and reading method (such as browser, Markdown or AI tools). These site-wide totals have no fixed retention period. We also count reads for each public article by day, reading method, and whether the original or a translation was shown. We do not link these article totals to readers’ accounts or devices. We use them to review site operations and to show counts to eligible signed-in members on article pages.

### What we do not measure

We do not use tracking cookies, stored IP addresses or device fingerprints to follow people who only read, and we do not count unique visitors. Existing authentication and abuse-prevention mechanisms are separate from reading counts. We do not use external analytics or advertising pixels. We do not record time spent reading, scrolling, completion or search terms. Our servers do not build a history of who read which articles, including for members.

Article-level counts are unavailable to signed-out readers. View counts do not affect the order of article lists. Operators can review article-level counts and reading trends to monitor site operations.

### Browser storage

To reduce repeated notices from the same tab, the browser stores the article, displayed language, whether it showed the original or a translation, and an expiry time in `sessionStorage`. Each record expires after 30 minutes. The browser does not send those stored records to the server.
