## What we do not measure

We do not track people who only read using tracking cookies, stored IP addresses or device fingerprints, and we do not count unique visitors. Existing authentication and abuse-prevention mechanisms are separate from reading counts. We do not use external analytics or advertising pixels. We do not record time spent reading, scrolling, completion or search terms. We do not build a history of who read which articles, including for members. We neither publish article-level view counts nor rank articles by them.
